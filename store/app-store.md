### App Review notes

```
Timegami needs no login: an anonymous account is created on first launch, after the privacy notice.

To try it:
1. Tap "New challenge", keep the "Every minute of the day" grid, give it a name and a nickname, tap Create.
2. Tap "It happened now!" — the cell for the current minute fills in. Tap any cell to see details or backfill it.
3. Switch between "My board", "Shared board" and "Heat map".

Shared challenges: you can join our demo room with the invite code [DEMO CODE] ("Join with code" on the home screen).

User-generated content (challenge names, nicknames, notes) is only visible inside private, invite-only rooms.
Moderation: in "Players", the "⋯" button next to another player opens "Report player"; the room owner also
gets "Remove from challenge" (removes the player and their marks, and renews the invite code). Non-owners
can use "Report challenge" at the bottom of the room screen. Reports go to enesgunel@enesgunel.com.
Account deletion: Settings → "Delete my account and data".
```
